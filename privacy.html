Privacy Policy

KazaAlkis is an opt-in informational Greek cultural calendar notification service providing Greek name days, Orthodox calendar information, holidays, and public-domain cultural content.

KazaALKIS collects only the information required to send requested WhatsApp calendar notifications, such as name, phone number, language preference, and opt-in status.

Contact details are used only for requested notifications after explicit consent. They are not sold, published on this website, used for unrelated marketing, or used for unsolicited spam messaging.

The public website contains calendar content only. Phone numbers, contact lists, API credentials, and private delivery logs are not published.

Users may unsubscribe at any time by sending STOP or contacting contact@kazaalkis.com.
