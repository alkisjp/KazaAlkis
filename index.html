KazaAlkis

KazaAlkis is a Greek daily calendar notification service providing name day reminders, public holiday notes, cultural greetings, and public-domain Greek proverbs through WhatsApp.

Service Purpose

  • Greek name day notifications
  • Daily cultural calendar reminders
  • Public holiday information
  • Greek proverbs and public-domain cultural notes

Users receive messages only after providing consent. Users may request removal from the notification list at any time.
